# Mechanical-Design-and-Control-Algorithms-for-Self-Balancing-Two-Wheeled-Vehicle
The project “Design of a Two-Wheeled Self-Balancing Vehicle” was carried out to study and develop a vehicle capable of maintaining balance while ensuring stability and safety during operation.

On the mechanical side, the vehicle structure was designed based on requirements such as a maximum load of 120 kg, a maximum speed of 15 km/h, and a climbing ability of 10°. Calculations and simulations were performed to determine motor torque, analyze stresses, and verify the strength of the main components. The results showed that all designed parts met the required strength and safety conditions.

On the control side, the system was modeled as an inverted pendulum. An LQR controller was used to maintain balance, while an adaptive PI controller was applied for motor control. A Kalman filter was also implemented to reduce sensor noise and improve feedback accuracy. MATLAB simulations demonstrated that the vehicle could maintain stable balance under different loading conditions with small angle oscillations.

The results indicate that the proposed self-balancing vehicle satisfies the design requirements in terms of mechanical structure, load capacity, and control performance.
